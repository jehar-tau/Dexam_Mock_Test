**MSQ partial-marking ladder (UCEED 2026, verbatim logic)** — verified, U26 §15:

| Condition | Marks |
|---|---|
| Exactly all correct options chosen, no wrong option | +4 |
| All four options correct, only three chosen, no wrong option | +3 |
| Three or more options correct, only two chosen (both correct), no wrong option | +2 |
| Two or more options correct, only one chosen (correct), no wrong option | +1 |
| Nothing chosen | 0 |
| Any other case (any wrong option chosen) | −1 |

### Owner's claim: "the 2026 Prelims was entirely drawing-based, with no aptitude section"

**Status: UNCONFIRMED by any official source.**

- The official 2026 handbooks neither confirm nor rule it out. They say only that questions "may involve text and visuals".
- NID does not publish actual Prelims papers. No official 2026 B.Des or M.Des paper was found. The local bank has no NID 2026 paper either.
- The **official sample paper linked during the 2026 cycle (D26SB) still has an objective Part I**: 1-mark MCQs (Q1–15), 2-mark questions (Q16–26) and a 3-mark question (Q27). Part II has 15-mark drawing tasks. That sample conflicts with the owner's claim, but a sample is not the real paper.
- Non-official reports **agree with the owner**. Careers360 and Shiksha post-exam analyses say there was no objective/Part A section and all questions were drawing/creative ([Careers360 analysis](https://design.careers360.com/articles/nid-dat-2026-exam-analysis), [Careers360 Prelims Part A](https://design.careers360.com/articles/nid-dat-2026-prelims-question-paper-part-a), [Shiksha B.Des analysis](https://www.shiksha.com/design/articles/nid-dat-2026-exam-analysis-for-b-des-blogId-216840)). These are coaching/news sources, so they count as **unverified**. One aggregator ([CollegeDekho](https://www.collegedekho.com/exam/nid-dat/paper-analysis)) also gives a wrong exam date.
- The rechecking rule (checking that all answers were marked and per-question totals were added) suits examiner-marked answer booklets. It does not prove there were no objective items.

**Recommendation:** keep two NID Prelims templates in config. Make the owner-confirmed "all-drawing" one the default for 2026, and keep a "mixed objective + drawing" one built from the official sample paper. Do not hard-code counts from either. Ask the owner for a candidate's copy or a recollection of the 2026 paper (task count, time, marks per task) before fixing the drawing template.
