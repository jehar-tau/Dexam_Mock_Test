# Storyboarding

Slug `storyboarding` · method pattern · deck `storyDeck` (`sb-`) · source:
the owner's storyboarding theory (method only; items our own).

Status: all four tiers **Built** (16 November 2026).

Steps taught: four-frame structure (setup, problem, twist, resolution),
shots (wide, close-up, reaction), captions that add what the drawing
cannot show, process sequences, movement (pendulum frames at equal time
steps, computed from a cosine), emotional journeys through posture and
setting, and critique. Ends with a checklist for the student's own
storyboard.
