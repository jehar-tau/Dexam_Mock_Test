# Posters, mascots and campaigns

Slug `posters` · method pattern · deck `posterDeck` (`pc-`) · source: the
owner's poster, mascot and campaign theory (method only; items our own).

Status: all four tiers **Built** (16 November 2026).

Steps taught: poster hierarchy (one message, one dominant visual, size
order), slogans (six to eight words, tone), mascots (value, traits,
silhouette), symbols and stamps (one form, works small and in one colour),
campaigns (audience, medium, tone, one action) and critique of common
mistakes. Ends with a checklist for the student's own poster.
